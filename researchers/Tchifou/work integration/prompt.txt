RESEARCH CONTEXT

We are studying the geometric structure of gender bias in static word embeddings.

The current project analyzes GloVe 6B 300d embeddings. A gender bias subspace is constructed using PCA on definitional gender word-pair differences (e.g., man–woman, he–she).

Key experiments already performed:

1. Progressive removal of the top k gender principal components.
2. Measurement of bias and geometry metrics:

   * Direct Bias (Bolukbasi et al., 2016)
   * WEAT association score (Caliskan et al., 2017)
   * Mean vector displacement after debiasing
   * Neighbor stability@10

Empirical observation so far:

* Removing PC1 almost completely eliminates Direct Bias.
* WEAT decreases more gradually.
* The semantic geometry (nearest neighbors) continues to change as additional PCs are removed.
* Single-PC ablation shows:

  * PC1 strongly affects Direct Bias
  * PC2 affects WEAT and semantic geometry more strongly.

Working hypothesis:

Gender bias in word embeddings is not captured by a single direction but rather by a low-dimensional subspace.

PROJECT GOAL

Investigate the geometric structure of bias in embedding spaces and understand how different bias metrics respond to components of the bias subspace.
